# Role rotation and handover

Course rules:

- roles rotate after every stage;
- nobody keeps the same role twice;
- a five-person team has one role shared by two named co-leads in each stage;
- a Lead coordinates an area, while everyone still contributes everywhere;
- individual contributions remain visible and are graded separately;
- members still own and repair their earlier work.

## Handover checklist

- [ ] Incoming person can find current work and decisions.
- [ ] Every unfinished task has an issue and owner.
- [ ] Risks and deadlines are explicit.
- [ ] No essential information exists only in private messages.
- [ ] README/report records the new assignment.
